# House style of fairness-opinion board decks: six banks compared
    
    Source: SC 13E-3 (c)-exhibit board/special-committee decks on EDGAR, 2019–2026. Each bank has 4–7 decks from different deals; the list and URLs are in `<bank>/sources.csv`, with a `usability` column. The full detail is in `<bank>.json`.
    
    How the readings were made: the decks are slide images (JPEG) inside the filed HTML. Colors were measured on solid fills. Typefaces were identified by eye, and point sizes were estimated from text height. Logos were not described or sampled; only where they sit is noted.
    
    Every bank uses more than one template. Each column below gives the bank's dominant recent template, and the variants are listed under it. "FF" means football field.

    ## Where decks within a bank disagree
    
    - **Goldman.** It uses three templates at the same time, and they do not line up with date: Janus (Mar 2026) still uses the classic Arial/navy template. The others are a widescreen Calibri-style template (5&2, Dec 2025) and a serif brand template (Select Medical, Mar 2026). The serif one appears in a single market-update deck, so its analysis pages have never been seen. The offer-line color and the confidentiality legend change from deck to deck. The `$ 45.14` / `7.9 x` spacing holds in all seven decks.
    - **Perella.** Page size differs (Eargo is 16:9), and so do the legends and the price-line colors. Baldwin (2026) adds "Title | Qualifier" titles and an FF Commentary column. Manitex was prepared for the buyer, uses an orange/gold palette and shows an offer *range*.
    - **J.P. Morgan.** There are three templates, one per era: a 2021 blue photo band with a vertical FF; 2022 gray titles under a steel-blue rule; and the 2025–26 brown accent rule. The FF and DCF conventions come only from Inovalon and Clearwater. The DSG deck carries no J.P. Morgan name; it is attributed to the bank only by the EDGAR exhibit index.
    - **Jefferies.** Focus (2023) is the odd one out. It has vertical method-colored FF bars, no interior "Confidential" and no disclaimer page. Carrols is 16:9 and mixes in a humanist sans. Reference bars are light blue in Whole Earth and gray elsewhere. The offer line is solid black, solid blue-gray or dashed gray, depending on the deck.
    - **Citi.**
      - Legacy template (2019, 2023): Arial, navy/cyan, page number bottom-left.
      - New template (Dec 2024 onward): brand sans, bright blue, "logo | page" footer bottom-right.
      - The two templates' FFs differ completely (see the table).
      - Only two of the six decks have an FF. Precedent-table style rests on Aspen alone.
    - **Evercore.** Diversey (2023), probably a London deal team, has no legend and bigger titles. It uses giant numeral dividers, a method-colored horizontal FF, minus-sign negatives and British spelling. FF orientation also splits between the US decks. The offer line is orange in two decks and red in two.
    
    ## Caveats
    
    - **Point sizes:** estimates, roughly ±2pt.
    - **Typefaces:** identified by eye from raster images.
      - High to medium-high confidence: Arial-like faces.
      - Medium confidence: Jefferies' Trade Gothic.
      - Low confidence: the name of Citi's new face.
    - **Thin lines:** price-line and hairline colors are anti-aliased in the JPEGs, so they are approximate. Solid fills are reliable.
    - **Goldman Enstar:** the filer re-typeset the deck as HTML, so it shows structure only.
    - **Short or partial decks:** Citi Aircastle (c)(5), Citi Whole Earth, Utz, J.P. Morgan Myovant and DSG, and Goldman Select Medical. They are kept for furniture and template evidence, as marked in `sources.csv`.
    - **EDGAR access:** nothing was blocked. Requests were spaced 0.5s apart, with a User-Agent of the form "fairness-opinion-legal research <contact>".
