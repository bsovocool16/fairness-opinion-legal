# Form notes by advisor (procedure mode)

Derived from the advisor's own filed sections, not from any deal being drafted. Used only in procedure mode.

The form is an assembly specification for same-advisor language. It is not a new generic fairness-opinion essay. Fixed text comes from identified paragraphs of an applicable precedent; slots carry current facts. Preserve retained paragraphs and their order after the structure has been selected. Do not use a test filing as a donor.

## Advisor notes from non-test precedents

Sources are local ids under the named bank. `NAMES.md` and `learning_evidence.json` contain the source inventory. The notes distinguish observed variants from requirements. A heading that begins “Summary of Financial Analyses” can be a partial cut; it does not establish that the advisor omits the opinion, review and reliance portions.

### William Blair (`williamblair`, 5 sections)

Sources: ARC 2024 (`13e3_arc_2024__william-blair-company-l-l-c`) and CASMED 2019 (`proxy_0001144204-19-013709__tv515314-defm14a`). Titles vary between the committee's financial advisor and the advisor's full name. Sequence: retention/request/conclusion; conspicuous all-capitals opinion notice; examined-document bullets; discussions; reliance and limitations; financial-analysis introduction; public companies; transactions; DCF; any LBO; premiums; General; Fees. The review list is a third-person rendering of letter facts in the “William Blair examined” frame. Preserve who prepared and approved forecasts and who consented to reliance. Public-company and transaction sections explain selection, metric construction, comparison to the offer and non-comparability. ARC uses peer multiples and separate offer-implied multiple/minimum/median/mean/maximum tables; CASMED separates micro-cap and small-cap statistics. DCF often states ranges in prose. ARC puts the reference LBO leverage case inside the LBO discussion. Neither source supports converting every reference item into a core valuation. Fees separate opinion payment, completion payment and credit; relationships precede Fees. Terms include stock class, excluded/rollover/dissenting holders, Merger Consideration and the applicable projection version.

### Kroll and Duff & Phelps (`kroll`, 25)

Sources: Fathom 2024, BEST 2024, and complete opinion frame `proxy_0001193125-23-276048__d446572ddefm14a`. Use the actual entity/practice identity; the library groups both brands and does not authorize changing a letter's entity. Full form: Opinion/Summary of Opinion; mandate and conclusion; notice and approval; procedures/inquiries list; reliance/assumption list; scope restrictions; summary and valuation analyses; conclusion/general and compensation where present. Letter procedures can be nested lists, and assumptions often are separately enumerated rather than compressed into one paragraph. Fathom uses Income Approach then Market Approach; this is a supported variant, not an order to impose on every book. Tables can include revenue/EBITDA growth, margins, operating groups, trading multiples, summary statistics, selected M&A and enterprise-to-equity bridges. Explain optionality/Black-Scholes when equity is negative and that method is actually used. Preserve separate scenarios, per-share/per-ADS units, adjustments and summary conclusions. Market observations may be contextual without a market-derived valuation. Fees/indemnity and independence require current sources. Terms include Management Projections, scenario names, Management Representation Letter, Terminal Value, Shares, ADSs and excluded-holder categories.

### Evercore (`evercore`, 49)

Usual sequence: engagement/opinion; annex/use notice; “In connection with rendering its opinion…” review bullets; reliance, transaction assumptions, inspection limitations and scope; summary; analyses; other factors; general and fees/relationships. The review list closely follows the letter while changing person, tense and proxy labels. Keep third-party appraisals and explicit reliance directions. GasLog has appraisal NAV before DCF, then market comparisons; choose by actual asset/deal structure. Tables identify companies/transactions, observed multiples, selected ranges and implied values, sometimes by forecast case. Do not confuse partnership units with common shares or a special distribution with total consideration. Reference targets/trading/NAV stay in their evidenced reference role. Fee paragraphs can distinguish retainer, opinion fee, completion fee and prior services. Terms include Conflicts Committee, Unaffiliated Unitholders, Consideration versus Merger Consideration, Forecasts, Appraisals, NAV, WACC and CAPM.

### Centerview (`centerview`, 83)

Sources: Tripadvisor/Liberty TripAdvisor 2025 and Agiliti 2024 (`13e3_1606745_2025__centerview-partners-llc`, `13e3_1749704_2024__centerview-partners-llc`). Title normally Opinion of Centerview [Partners LLC]. Opinion and use notice precede review bullets; the lead is “In connection with rendering the opinion described above and performing its related financial analyses…”. Follow with discussions, broad information reliance, direction/consent, transaction assumptions and scope. The letter's Forecasts may be nested within Internal Data; they are not interchangeable. Public-company/transaction analyses contain selection and non-comparability explanations, observed multiples, selected ranges and equity bridges; DCF follows in these donors. ### Goldman Sachs (`goldman`, 84)

Sources: Myovant and Covetrus plus `proxy_0001104659-26-011567__tm264092-1_defm14a`. Full titles identify the financial advisor; the examples often start at the financial summary. The complete frame uses short opinion/annex paragraphs, review bullets, discussions, “For purposes of rendering its opinion…” reliance, limitations and fairness-committee approval, then analyses. Preserve letter facts without duplicating its opening transaction recital. Historical/implied premia may precede illustrative DCF; future-share-price and transaction analyses are conditional. Show metric periods and selected ranges, separately identify standalone/synergy or segment cases, and retain “illustrative” qualifications. Transaction tables can be grouped by industry; footnotes explain metric adjustments and rounding. “Other Information” contains reference targets/trading data in Covetrus. “Other Written Presentations” describes earlier materials separately; it is not a license to reproduce every preliminary sensitivity as a current analysis. Closing paragraphs cover broad securities/investment activities, actual prior relationships, selection rationale and fee/credit/discretionary components. Projection names, NOL Forecasts, transaction/stock labels, NTM and LTM require precise definitions.

### Moelis (`moelis`, 25)

## General output form and slots

Assemble once, before filling. Every paragraph gets an id and source locator in the log; ids are not printed in the draft.

```text
[Advisor/recipient-specific section title]
[Retained mandate/request/conclusion paragraph: current entity, recipient, opinion date,
 written/oral sequence only if supported, exact subject consideration and beneficiaries.]
[Retained full-opinion/annex/use/no-recommendation notice.]
[Retained review-list lead.]
[Each current reviewed document/procedure in the donor's list syntax.]
[Retained discussions paragraph.]
[Retained reliance, forecasts, transaction assumptions, limitations and approval paragraphs,
 each filled once from the letter; preserve distinct consents and exceptions.]
[Retained summary/whole-analysis/table-reading/data-date paragraph(s).]
[Supported metric/forecast/capitalization assumptions before the analyses.]
[Applicable core analysis modules in selected advisor/structure order.]
[Reference/other-information modules, in their evidenced location.]
[Separate history of preliminary presentations, only if supplied and applicable.]
[Retained general/negotiation/selection statements with only supported current facts.]
[Retained fee, reimbursement, indemnity, relationships and securities-activity paragraphs
 in the donor's order.]
```

An analysis module contains selection/universe and data dates; methodology and definitions; complete observations; selected valuation assumptions; application to the subject company/case; enterprise-to-equity bridge and denominator when supplied; resulting ranges and comparison to consideration; limitations. Do not infer that an observed multiple was applied in valuation. Reference modules describe only the role and results actually supported. The template's square brackets are drafting slots, never output text.

## Table forms

Use the chosen donor's captions, precision, row order, units, grouping and footnote placement. These schemas specify contents, not compulsory extra metrics.

```text
Selected company | [EV/unit if shown] | [metric A / period] | [metric B / period] ...
[every selected peer; each group retained]
[Minimum / Median / Mean / Maximum or percentile rows IF present in the source]

Announcement/completion date [label exact] | Target | Acquiror | [EV/unit] | [metric(s)]
[every selected transaction; distinct business acquisitions remain distinct]
[summary rows if supplied; subject transaction separately labeled if shown]

Case / metric / segment | Observed range [if supplied] | Selected range | Implied value low | high
[one row per applicable case/method; Overall only with supported combination rule]

Discount rate | [terminal assumption 1] | [terminal assumption 2] ...
[every row of a carried sensitivity table; source precision retained]

Period / benchmark | Subject premium | [25th / median / mean / 75th or other source percentiles]
[all source observation periods in a carried premiums table]

Analysis / case | Implied per-share range | Consideration [correct cash/CVR/stock basis]
```

Write footnotes immediately below their table, with every marker resolved. Preserve NM/NA/dashes and their meanings; missing and zero are different. Retain source statistics rather than recomputing a displayed median from rounded rows. Carry all peer and transaction entries and their supplied multiples. Other supporting tables can be represented in the advisor's prose form only when every disclosed result is preserved and the log maps the source entries. Raw management forecast tables, WACC inputs and historical panels must be inventoried even when they are supporting inputs rather than publication tables. Never silently treat a clipped table as complete.
